# When art becomes a source of warmth
## Art of Meaning × ArtisHeat®

Bespoke murals, sculptural forms and heating integrated into one wall composition.

This collaboration between Art of Meaning and ArtisHeat® brings painting together with a decorative heating element. The subject, scale and character of each composition respond to a particular space — its architecture, function and atmosphere. The heating element is integrated into the artistic form, while optional lighting highlights its texture.

### One wall, three functions

**Art.** A bespoke mural and sculptural form. The artistic direction may draw on landscape, nature, the human figure or classical architecture.

**Warmth.** An electric ArtisHeat® heating element selected for the needs of the room. Output, dimensions and controls are determined during technical specification.

**Light.** Optional LED backlighting that reveals the relief and shapes the atmosphere of the interior.

### Suitable spaces

Private interiors and hospitality settings: living rooms, entrance halls, apartments, hotels, lobbies and spa areas. The composition can become the main focal point or part of the interior's wider visual story.

### Scope of collaboration

- a consultation about the space and the desired result;
- concept design and visualisations;
- selection of the heating solution;
- production and installation within the agreed scope.

Art of Meaning contributes the artistic direction and painting; ArtisHeat® provides the heating solution integrated into the composition. Each party's detailed scope is set out in the individual proposal.

### The process

1. Send photographs of the wall, approximate dimensions, location and details of the space.
2. We discuss the artistic direction, budget and target schedule.
3. We prepare the concept and specify the heating solution.
4. We agree the final scope, quotation and delivery schedule.

### Budget

Compositions from **€10,000**, as stated in the supplied offer. The final price depends on the scale of the painting, complexity of the form, required heating output and scope of work. Individually quoted.
