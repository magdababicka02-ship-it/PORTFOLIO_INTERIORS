## Suggested public copy (EN)
### Ceremonial Arrival
**Three visions for a Marbella entrance hall**

An entrance conceived as a moment of arrival. Developed during the concept phase for the NOVA KASYANOV design team, this private residential study explores three interpretations of Italian-inspired grandeur — from expressive ornament to restrained symmetry and dramatic contrast.

The proposals investigate a monumental double staircase, architectural symmetry and decorative identity through a palette of warm stone, metallic accents and contrasting textiles. Rather than presenting a single finished solution, the project documents a creative exploration of spatial character and atmosphere.

**Role:** Concept development · alternative design proposals · material palette research · moodboards · architectural visualisations.

**Project status:** Conceptual study; three proposed directions.

**Credit:** Concept work for NOVA KASYANOV · Private villa, Marbella, Spain.
